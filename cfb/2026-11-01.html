Big games this week

  • No. 2 Ohio State at USC without starting QB Julian Sayin. USC's playoff hopes are close to gone after a 24-7 loss at Wisconsin, and Lincoln Riley is facing questions about his future.
  • No. 3 Georgia vs No. 17 Florida moves from Jacksonville to Atlanta (Mercedes-Benz Stadium, indoors) for the first time.
  • No. 22 Arizona at No. 13 Texas Tech, and No. 20 Northwestern at Oregon.
  • No. 6 Utah (13 straight wins, the longest active FBS streak) at Cincinnati, which beat then-No. 8 Texas Tech 37-24 last week.

News and injuries

Coaching changes

  • Maryland fired Mike Locksley after a 2-5 start, and Aazaar Abdul-Rahim is interim coach. Illinois visits this week.
  • NC State fired Dave Doeren. Todd Goebbel coaches his first game as interim against Cal.
  • Baylor fired Dave Aranda (Joe Klanderman interim), and Sam Houston fired Phil Longo.
  • Oregon fired offensive coordinator Drew Mehringer this week, and Jay Johnson takes over play-calling.

Quarterback news

  • Out: Julian Sayin (OSU, arm, likely rest of regular season), Dante Moore (ORE, concussion, season), Avery Johnson (KSU), Mason Heintschel (PITT, ACL, season), Kamario Taylor (MSST, toe, season), Nico Iamaleava (UCLA, knee, season), Carter Jones (NEV, arm).
  • Doubtful: Jacob Knuth (KSU, ankle) and Katin Houser (ILL, broken hand).
  • Questionable or game-time: Mikey Keene (Arizona State at BYU, leg) and Demond Williams Jr. (WASH, rib, upgraded to probable but no starter named).
  • Playing hurt: Indiana QB Josh Hoover injured his right arm against Ohio State and has played through it.
  • Texas is without LT Trevor Goosby (season), and edge Colin Simmons was doubtful midweek.

Five plays

1. Kansas State @ Colorado: Under 46.5

Kansas State is on its third quarterback in three weeks. Avery Johnson is out, Jacob Knuth is doubtful, and Blake Barnett is expected to start. Without Johnson, K-State scored 17 at Arizona State. Colorado's offense ranks 120th in scoring (20.1 per game), and against FBS teams it has scored 14, 7, 13, 7, 24 and 24. K-State ranks 17th in time of possession (32:38) and leans on the run, which shortens the game. My rough projection is low 40s. AccuWeather has a 51% chance of precipitation at about 13°C in Boulder.

Risk: Colorado's defense is poor (112th in scoring) and its -9 turnover margin (135th) can hand out short fields. Its last game, a 57-24 loss to Oklahoma State, totalled 81. The total has already dropped from 48.5.
