Division leaders

  • AFC: Bills 5-1 (East), Steelers and Browns both 5-2 (North), Jaguars 4-2 (South), Chiefs 5-1 (West).
  • NFC: Giants 4-2-1 (East), Vikings 6-0 (North), Falcons and Panthers 3-4 (South), 49ers 6-1 (West).

Surprises and disappointments

  • Vikings 6-0 on defence alone: 74 points allowed and the best FPI defense, but an FPI offense of -3.4.
  • Browns 5-2 despite a -13 point differential, tied for the AFC North lead.
  • Giants lead the NFC East at 4-2-1 with only a -4.3 FPI.
  • Burrow and Caleb Williams are both at season highs in NFL.com's QB Index.
  • Chargers 0-6 with a league-low 15 points a game; OC Mike McDaniel's job was in question over the bye.
  • Eagles 2-5 with five straight losses since Hurts went down.
  • Commanders 1-4-1 (-40), Dolphins 1-5 (-59), Cardinals 2-5 (-66, worst in the league).
  • Defending champion Seahawks are 4-3, though their +43 differential is fourth-best.

Strength indicators

  • Best point differentials: SF +58, MIN +51, JAX +50, SEA +43, CIN +41, KC +40.
  • FPI top five: 49ers +5.9 (22.7% to win the Super Bowl, the clear favourite), Bills +4.3, Seahawks +4.3, then Chiefs, Bengals and Jaguars at +3.9.
  • Best FPI offenses: BUF +5.8, SF +5.1, DAL +4.3, CIN +4.1. Best FPI defenses: MIN +4.1, SEA +3.6, DEN +2.4, LAR +2.1.
  • Unlucky records: TB 2-5 at +11 and LAR 3-4 at +11. Lucky ones: CLE 5-2 at -13 and NYG 4-2-1 at +2.

News and injuries

Coaching and front office

  • No head coach has been fired in-season in anything I found. The Colts fired GM Chris Ballard on 19 Oct after a 2-4 start, and Shane Steichen stays as coach.
  • Hot seats per CBS and PFT: Aaron Glenn (Jets), Shane Steichen (Colts), Dan Quinn (Commanders). Chargers OC McDaniel is under fire, but Jim Harbaugh is still backing him.
  • First-year coaches in this slate include Jesse Minter (Ravens), Kevin Stefanski (Falcons), Todd Monken (Browns), Robert Saleh (Titans) and Mike LaFleur (Cardinals).

Trades (deadline Tue 10 Nov 4pm ET, Wed 11 Nov 8am AEDT)

  • Bears get DT Jeffery Simmons from the Titans for 2027 and 2028 first-round picks plus a 2027 fourth. ESPN grades it Bears C, Titans A.
  • 49ers get DT DeForest Buckner from the Colts for a 2027 fourth.
  • Seahawks get G Quenton Nelson from the Colts for a 2027 third and a 2028 fifth. Indianapolis is now openly rebuilding.
  • Earlier: Arizona traded WR Marvin Harrison Jr., who is now catching passes from C.J. Stroud in Houston.

QBs, major injuries and suspensions

  • Jalen Hurts (PHI) is on IR with a deep vein thrombosis and clots in both lungs, likely out for the season. Tanner McKee starts.
  • Lamar Jackson (BAL) missed three games with a high ankle sprain and practised fully Wednesday and Thursday.
  • No starting QB has been benched yet this season (NFL.com). Kirk Cousins stays the Raiders' starter over Fernando Mendoza.
  • Season-ending or long-term IR: Terry McLaurin (WSH, knee), Laremy Tunsil (WSH, triceps), Erick All (CIN, ACL), Anton Harrison (JAX, ACL). Davante Adams (LAR) had hamstring surgery, and Micah Parsons (GB, ACL) may practise next week.
  • Suspensions: Bills S C.J. Gardner-Johnson is out one game for spitting. Bears edge Montez Sweat is back from a two-game ban for Monday night.

Practice notes are from Wednesday and Thursday. The final Friday injury reports, with official Out, Doubtful and Questionable tags, come out on Saturday morning AEDT, so check those before betting.
